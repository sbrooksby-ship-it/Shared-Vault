# Reading Log

*Hands you've studied. This is where the skill actually builds — reading notes are knowledge, but reading a hand in order, top to bottom, over and over, is the practice.*

**How I file each reading:** one `## ` section per hand, with the date and who it was (or "mine" / "practice hand"), then the read in order. Full detail goes in `05 - Practice/` as a note per hand once we have more than a handful.
