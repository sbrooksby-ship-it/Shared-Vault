## How I file things
So you know where to look and what to expect:

- **Lessons** → `01 - Lessons/` — one note per session, numbered.
- **Major Lines** → `02 - Concepts/` — one note per line (Life, Head, Heart, Fate, Sun, Mercury). Each holds the full traditional meaning, what to look for, and what your course adds.
- **Shapes & Skin** → `02 - Concepts/` — hand shape and skin type, read *before* the lines.
- **Reference** → `03 - Reference/` — the mounts, minor lines and markings, books and teachers.
- **Practice** → `Reading Log` — hands you've studied. The actual skill-builder.
- **Reflections** → `04 - Reflections/` — your journal. Never restructured or rewritten, just filed and linked.
- **Questions** → `04 - Reflections/Questions.md` — anything unanswered.

Every note carries **tags** (`#palmistry/lesson`, `#palmistry/line`, `#palmistry/reference`, `#palmistry/journal`, `#palmistry/questions`) so the tag pane and search both work as shortcuts into the course.

## A note on how this is recorded
Palmistry is a traditional interpretive practice, not a validated science — it's not something I want to record as if it were proven fact. So in these notes I'm careful to:

- Attribute meanings to their **sources** (classical Western / Indian *Hasta Samudrika* / your specific teacher) rather than stating them as universal truth.
- Mark **disagreements** between traditions instead of flattening them into one answer.
- Separate **what a tradition claims** from **what's observationally true** (e.g. that lines change with age is observable; that they predict events is a claim).

If your course teaches something as fact that I record as tradition, tell me and I'll match your course's framing — it's your notes, your call.
