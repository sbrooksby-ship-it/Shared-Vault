# Reference — The Mounts

**One-sentence definition:** Mounts are the **raised, fleshy pads** across the palm, each named for one of the seven classical planets. They show *disposition* — the lean or tendency of a quality — as opposed to the lines, which show *what's actually been expressed*.

## Why they matter
A prominent mount doesn't mean you *have* that quality — it means the quality is **available and easily expressed**. A flat mount doesn't mean you lack it; it means you have to work at it. This distinction is the backbone of reading mounts correctly.

## The map

```

              |        |        |       |
          JUPITER   SATURN   APOLLO  MERCURY      ← under each finger

              UPPER MOUNT OF MARS (in the palm, between

              under a finger)
              ────────────────────────────────────
     VENUS  ╱              (the plain of Mars)
   (thumb) ╱    LUNA  (the fleshy outer edge under pinky)
```

## The seven classical mounts

| Mount | Where | Traditionally governs |
|-------|-------|----------------------|
| **Jupiter** | base of the index finger | expansion, authority, ambition, leadership, confidence |
| **Saturn** | base of the middle finger | responsibility, discipline, patience, isolation, self-control |
| **Apollo (Sun)** | base of the ring finger | creativity, warmth, fame, public success, joy |
| **Mercury** | base of the little finger | communication, commerce, intellect, adaptability |
| **Luna (Moon)** | fleshy outer edge below the pinky | imagination, intuition, sensitivity, dreams, the subconscious |
| **Venus** | the big pad at the base of the thumb | love, passion, beauty, vitality, sensuality |
| **Mars** | two areas — upper (in the palm) and lower (near the wrist) | drive, courage, anger, desire, physical force |

## How to read a mount
The vocabulary is consistent: **prominent** (raised, full), **moderate** (average), **flat** (barely there), **pinched** (constrained by lines nearby), **wide**, **high**.

| You observe | It reads as |
|---|---|
| Prominent | the quality is *easily available*, often expressed |
| Flat | the quality needs deliberate effort to express — not absent, just untrained |
| **Pinched / crowded by lines** | the quality is *present but under pressure* — often described as the most interesting case; suppressed or channeled |
| Well-defined edge | the quality is consistent and reliable |
| Very wide | an emphasis or excess of that quality |
| High position (toward the fingers) | mentalized version of the quality — *thinking about* it rather than living it |
| Low position (toward the wrist) | instinctive version — *living* it rather than thinking about it |

## The two Mars mounts are different things
Most beginners merge these and lose accuracy:

- **Upper Mars** — in the palm's "plain of Mars," between Saturn and Apollo: **courage, drive, assertiveness, the will to act**
- **Lower Mars** — near the wrist, below Venus: **physical resilience, stamina, anger, raw physical desire, temper**

## Reading the whole pattern
Courses teach you to **never read a single mount alone** — look at the pattern across all seven. A flat Mercury under a prominent Jupiter is a different person than a prominent Mercury with everything else flat. The pattern is the reading; the individual mount is only a data point.

## ⚠️ Health note
Classical texts assign bodily organs to mounts (Venus → kidneys/reproductive, Mercury → lungs/nerves, Jupiter → liver, etc.) and read health from their development. As with the Life line, I've noted the tradition without treating it as diagnostic.
