## Where traditions differ — worth reading side by side
- **Western classical** — the 19th-century British/American lineage. Emphasises mounts, shape, and the "signatures." Often reads a downturned Heart line as melancholy.
- **Indian (Hasta Samudrika Shastra)** — assigns planets/attributes differently, often reads "low" or flat features as *gifts* rather than deficits, and has a far more elaborate mount vocabulary.
- Note they disagree on the **Mount of Luna vs. Moon**, the meaning of the **Ring of Venus/Saturn**, and the direction of several line endings. I'll flag these as they come up.
