# Reference — Minor Lines & Markings

**One-sentence definition:** The detail layer — the lines and marks that most beginners never learn, and the difference between reading a hand like a checklist and reading it like a person.

## Minor lines

| Line | Where | Traditional meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Girdle of Venus** | curved line(s) crossing under the middle and ring fingers | emotional sensitivity, self-discipline, love of beauty. Two or more = strongly self-controlled about feeling |
| **Bracelets / Raschette** | horizontal lines across the **wrist** | health and longevity. One or two = good vitality. Many, fine, clear ones = strength; broken or irregular = health that's fluctuates |
| **Simian line** | a single horizontal line running *across the whole palm* instead of a separate Head and Heart line | concentration, intensity, a "single-track" focus. Traditionally read as a strong determinist or a lucky gambler's hand; classically considered a sign worth noting, not negative |
| **Line of Intuition** | crescent inside the thumb, between Luna and Mercury | intuition, imagination; when strong, often said to compensate for a weaker Head line |
| **Marriage / Affection lines** | short lines on the outer edge under the pinky | traditionally read as **deep attachments** and how you bond — modern courses are careful here, since "marriage lines" often get over-read into fixed predictions |
| **Line of Mars** | a bold diagonal from the base of the thumb or Luna across to the mount of Saturn | drive, courage, physical force, sometimes read as an accident-prone crossing of the Life line. See the caveat in [[Line - Life]] |

## Markings

| Mark | Traditional meaning |
|---|---|
| **Stars** | four-pointed asterisks. Traditionally a mark of **good luck / protection**; classically called *four-leaved stars* and considered among the most auspicious signs |
| **Crosses** | a tiny cross on a mount or line — traditionally a warning: illness, sadness, obstruction. Modern courses caution heavily against reading these as predictions |
| **Bars** | short horizontal lines *crossing* a major line — interference, a setback, someone crossing your path. Often also a sign of a period of *temporary* difficulty that resolves |
| **Dots** | raised small points — classically a period of happiness or a fortunate moment in that area |
| **T's / 7's** | the line of the Sun and the line of Saturn crossing the head line — conventionally a sign of an important life moment / a break in circumstances |

## ⚠️ The honesty caveat
This is the category where palmistry's claims are weakest and where experienced readers most often over-interpret. Small marks shift with lighting, dirt, dry skin, and how the hand is held. Courses that are honest about this will:

- Tell you to **wash and dry the hand** before reading, and check a mark from more than one angle
- Treat **stars as genuine**, but treat cross/bar interpretations as the *most disputed* part of the practice
- Encourage reading the **pattern over time** (does a bar persist across weeks?) rather than a single snapshot
