# Line — Life

**One-sentence definition:** The Life line shows **vitality and health** — not how long you live, but how *well* and how *fully* you live the years you have.

> This is the line beginners most often misread as a lifespan meter. Most courses explicitly say it does **not** predict how long you'll live.

## Where it runs
Starts near the wrist between the thumb and index finger, arcs up and around the base of the thumb, and usually ends near the thumb's base on the thumb side. It should **curve** — it encircles the Venus mount. A line that runs almost straight down from the wrist is read differently.

## What to observe

| Feature | Reads as |
|---|---|
| **Depth & clarity** | vitality. Deep and clear = strong constitution; faint = lower reserve, needs more rest |
| **Length** | how far it runs. A short one does *not* mean short life — it reads as vitality concentrated earlier, or energy spent on certain areas |
| **Width** | a wide Life line reads as physical, robust, endurance-first |
| **Curvature** | deep curve around Venus = strongly physical, sensual, embodied. Straight = less driven by physical sensation |
| **Evenness** | an even line reads steadier than one that varies in depth along its run |

## Classical signs worth knowing

| Sign | Traditional meaning |
|---|---|
| **Chain / bead-like** | segmented line — vitality that comes in bursts; periods of lows and highs |
| **Double Life line** | a fainter parallel line — read variously as resilience/a backup, or extra physical drive |
| **Barelled / beads on a string** | sharper fluctuations than a chain |
| **Line of Mars crossing it** | a bold diagonal cutting the Life line — traditionally a sign of *interference*, an accident or a shock. **Note:** most modern courses explicitly caution against reading this as a prediction of physical harm. |
| **Long hooked terminal, sweeping to Jupiter** | strongly physical, robust constitution |
| **Ends at Jupiter** | traditionally read as a sign to do with *will and drive running into later life* |

## ⚠️ Health claims
Palmistry's health readings are its weakest area — the tradition assigns meanings (kidneys, gallbladder, nerves, circulation) that don't map to how modern medicine works. I've recorded what the tradition says, but I won't present it as diagnostic. If you learn this for readings, most responsible practitioners frame the Life line as **"general vitality and how you live"** rather than predicting illness or lifespan.
