# Line — Fate (Saturn)

**One-sentence definition:** The Fate line shows **career, direction, and how much of your life is in your own hands**. Present in roughly half of all hands — its absence is a reading, not a failure.

## Where it runs
A vertical line from the wrist (often from the line of Mars) up toward the **middle finger**. It may stop short of the finger or run all the way.

## What to observe

| Feature | Reads as |
|---|---|
| **Absent** | traditionally read as not your own life; circumstances, others, or circumstance set the direction. Frequently paired with a strong Head line as the compensating strength |
| **Present and strong** | driven, career-conscious, wants to run things |
| **From the wrist, straight up** | controlled, self-directed, a life deliberately managed |
| **Starting mid-palm, not at the wrist** | enters the picture mid-life; direction becomes clearer later |
| **From the Life line, not the wrist** | the body/instinct and the career direction are aligned — the two agree |
| **Deep, ending under the middle finger** | conventionally the most "developed" placement |
| **Wavering / braided line** | changes of direction, career shifts; not necessarily negative |
| **Cut by the Head line** | career and intellect at odds; thinking about wanting to do something else |
| **Via Combusta** (dark, crossed line) | classical term for a "burnt" line — traditionally read as a hard break in career. **Note:** a darkening that appears on one reading and fades on the next is often just dirt or lighting, not a marking |

## The "half of all hands" caveat
Most sources put the Fate line's presence at roughly 40–50%. So an absent Fate line is *common*, not exceptional — and the reading of it (circumstances vs. choice) is heavily interpretive and disputed even among palmists.
