# Line — Heart

**One-sentence definition:** The Heart line shows **emotion and how you love** — what you value, what moves you, and how you invest emotionally.

> It's called the *Heart* line but it governs emotion, not the organ.

## Where it runs
Starts under the index finger at the Jupiter mount and runs **across** the palm toward the outer edge. It's the **first horizontal line** on the palm. Where it *ends* is the single most-read feature.

## What to observe

| Feature | Reads as |
|---|---|
| **Ending: under the pinky** (long) | emotionally invested, gives deeply, relationships matter enormously |
| **Ending: under the middle finger** | balanced between feeling and thinking — the most emotionally stable placement |
| **Ending: under the index** | self-focused; emotion filtered through personal ambition or one's own convictions |
| **Curving up toward the base of the fingers** | optimism, emotional expressiveness, warmth |
| **Curving down toward the lower palm** | reserved, controlled, holds feelings close; classically read as a tendency toward melancholy |
| **Two Heart lines (double)** | rarely stated outright, but strongly associated with **good emotional capacity** — able to hold a lot without breaking |
| **Straight vs. curved** | straight = pragmatic about emotion; curved = feeling-led |

## The two kinds of Heart line

| | **Curved (watery)** | **Straight (level)** |
|---|---|---|
| Reads as | feeling-led, expressive, empathetic | practical about emotion, decides before feeling, steady under stress |

## ⚠️ Where traditions disagree
Classical Western palmistry reads a Heart line curving *down* as a sign of grief or melancholy, while Indian practice (Hasta Samudrika) tends to read the same placement as **emotional depth and sensitivity** — a strength rather than a liability. Ask me to record whichever way your course teaches it.
