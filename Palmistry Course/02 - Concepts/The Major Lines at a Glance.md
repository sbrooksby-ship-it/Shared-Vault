# The Major Lines at a Glance

**One-sentence definition:** Three lines are *always* present — **Life, Head, Heart** — and the rest are variable, present only in some hands. That distinction matters: you can't read a line that isn't there.

## The big picture

*Palm laid out like a clock face. Thumb side on the left if you're reading a right hand palm-up.*

```
          Index   Middle   Ring   Pinky
            |       |       |      |
        Jupiter Saturn Apollo Mercury     ← mounts sit under each finger
        ─────────────────────────────────
              ♥  HEART LINE
         ─── HEAD LINE ───
        ╱
       ╱  LIFE LINE
      ╱   (arcs around the thumb)
   ───────────────────────────────
        VENUS          wrist
```

## The three that always exist

| Line | Where it runs | Traditionally governs | Full note |
|------|---------------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Life** | starts near the wrist between thumb and index, arcs up and around the thumb, usually ends near the base of the thumb | vitality, health, natural lifespan, how you *live* your years | [[Line - Life]] |
| **Head** | starts *touched to* the Life line, runs across the palm toward the outer edge | mind, reasoning, how you think, your mental style | [[Line - Head]] |
| **Heart** | starts under the index finger at the Jupiter mount, runs *across* the palm to the outer edge | emotion, love, relationships, what you *value* | [[Line - Heart]] |

## The three that are variable

Present in many hands, absent in plenty of others. **Absent is meaningful, not a gap.**

| Line | Where it runs | Traditionally governs | Full note |
|------|---------------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Fate** (Saturn) | vertical, from the wrist up toward the middle finger | career, direction, whether you're the one in charge of your own path | [[Line - Fate]] |
| **Sun** (Apollo) | vertical, up under the ring finger | success, fame, recognition, creative/public warmth | [[Line - Sun]] |
| **Mercury** | short, under the little finger | communication, travel, nervous system, the restless mind | [[Line - Mercury]] |

## Read in this order
A reliable sequence, and the one most courses teach:

1. **Hand shape & skin** — the frame. → [[Hand Shapes & Skin Type]]
2. **Thumb** — will and how you approach. → [[The Thumb]]
3. **Life line** — the base, literal and physical
4. **Head line** — the mind
5. **Heart line** — the emotional life
6. **Fate / Sun** — direction and outward success
7. **Mounts** — the seven planets, the character layer. → [[Reference - The Mounts]]
8. **Minor lines & markings** — the detail that separates a beginner read from a real one

## Two hands
- **Dominant hand** (the one you write with) — traditionally the **self**; what you're actively doing now.
- **Non-dominant** — circumstances, inherited material, what comes *to* you, and the more innate/fixed layer.

Both are usually read. When they disagree, that's usually meaningful — it reads as *tension between the two*, not as an error to be averaged away.
