# Line — Sun (Apollo)

**One-sentence definition:** The Sun line shows **success, reputation, and creative or public warmth** — being liked, being noticed, being known for something.

## Where it runs
A vertical line running up under the **ring finger**, from the lower palm. It usually runs alongside the Fate line rather than alone.

## What to observe

| Feature | Reads as |
|---|---|
| **Absent** | not driven by fame or public recognition; success defined privately rather than outwardly. Very common |
| **Present, ending under the ring finger** | the classic placement — recognition through creative or joyful expression |
| **Ends under the middle finger** | conventionally read as **stronger**: the "supernatural" placement in classical text |
| **Straight and clear** | steady, reliable public presence |
| **Curved** | softer, more people-pleasing style of recognition |
| **Deep, clear, straight from the wrist** | warmth that draws people; a genuinely liked person |
| **Forked at the top** | the line splitting at the top — classically read as success *not* reached, or as influence that divides |
| **Short** | recognition comes but doesn't stay; brief notability |

## Sun line vs. Fate line
The two are read as a pair, because they usually run together:

| | **Fate line** | **Sun line** |
|---|---|---|
| Governs | direction, whether you steer | recognition, whether you're liked |
| Question it answers | *Am I in charge?* | *Will people respond to me?* |

A strong Fate with no Sun reads as someone who *leads* but isn't broadly celebrated. A Sun with no Fate reads as someone who's liked and visible without necessarily being the one directing things.

## ⚠️ Caveat
"Success" here means the tradition's concept of it — public regard, being known. Courses vary a lot on how *much* weight the Sun line carries. Several modern courses treat it as one of the most over-read features and advise reading it lightly.
