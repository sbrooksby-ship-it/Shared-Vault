# Line — Head

**One-sentence definition:** The Head line shows **the mind** — how you think, your mental style, and where your attention naturally lands.

## Where it runs
Starts **touched to** the Life line (they should just meet, not float apart) and runs across the palm toward the outer edge. Most courses say: if the Head line starts *away from* the Life line, there's a meaningful gap — the mind running more independently of the body/instinct.

## What to observe

| Feature | Reads as |
|---|---|
| **Ending: under the pinky** | logical, analytical, methodical — processes things step by step |
| **Ending: under the middle finger** | practical, sensible, "middle of the road" reasoning |
| **Ending: under the index** | intuitive, creative, big-picture; less interested in detail |
| **Straight** | concrete, logical, decisive thinking |
| **Sloping down toward Luna** | imaginative, creative, a mind that reaches for meaning and pictures |
| **Sloping up** | practical, grounded, focused on facts and usefulness |
| **Very long, running off the palm** | restless, curious, many interests at once |
| **Short Head line** | not the sharpest analytical tool; decides fast, thinks physically |

## Where it joins the Life line
A course commonly teaches this as the first check:
- **Starts on the Life line** — mind and body aligned; thinks with the gut, feels first
- **Starts *below* the Life line, joined** — mind and body in partnership, a deliberate balance
- **Starts *above*, separated** — the mind overrides instinct; analysis over feeling
- **Starts well below** — the mind works against the body's instincts; hesitation

## Where it joins the Heart line
If the Head and Heart lines **meet or cross**, that's a whole-life balance — reason and emotion integrated. **Separate and parallel** reads as feeling and thinking operating as separate systems, which is the common case and considered entirely normal.
