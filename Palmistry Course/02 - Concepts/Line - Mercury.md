# Line — Mercury

**One-sentence definition:** The Mercury line shows **communication, travel, and the quality of your nervous-system wiring** — how you get information out and in, and how much you move.

## Where it runs
A short line **under the little finger**, usually on the outer edge of the palm below the Mercury mount. It's the *least* consistently drawn line of the six — many hands have no clear one.

## What to observe

| Feature | Reads as |
|---|---|
| **Absent** | doesn't travel much, or stays local; thinks before speaking |
| **Present, ending at the Mercury mount** | the standard placement — communicates easily, moves around |
| **Straight** | direct, clear speaker/writer; says what they mean |
| **Curved** | persuasive, diplomatic, bends language to the audience |
| **Forked** | classically read as a **double tongue** — persuasive to the point of being able to argue both sides; some traditions read it as a warning sign of untrustworthiness, others as negotiation skill. Disputed |
| **Deep and clear** | a quick, precise mind and speech |
| **Wavy** | restless, changeable, thinks out loud constantly |

## The Mercury mount vs. the Mercury line
A distinction most beginner courses get wrong:

| | **Mount of Mercury** | **Mercury line** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | the raised pad under the pinky | the line under the pinky |
| Reads as | the **disposition** toward communication and commerce | the **degree that disposition has been exercised** |

Same pattern as Jupiter and the Sun line: the mount is the tendency, the line is the actual track record.

## Travel & "stam"
Many courses frame the Mercury line as the travel line — how much a person ranges, moves, or shifts. Hand shape feeds this: a Fire hand with a strong Mercury line reads as movement and restlessness; the same Mercury line in a Water hand reads more as talkative/emotional exchange.
