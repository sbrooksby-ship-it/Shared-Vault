# Course Architecture (Jonny's running notes)

*The system I file into. Read this if you want to change how things are organized.*

## Folders & files

| Where | Holds |
|---|---|
| `Palmistry Course - Home.md` | the front door — every note is linked from here |
| `00 - Course Overview.md` | the facts about the course + how I record things |
| `01 - Lessons/` | one note per lesson, numbered so they sort in order |
| `02 - Concepts/` | the substance: one note per line, plus shape/thumb/framework |
| `03 - Reference/` | mounts, minor lines & markings, books & teachers |
| `04 - Reflections/` | your journal + open questions |
| `Reading Log.md` | one section per hand studied |
| `Templates/` | the blank shapes I fill in |

## The rules I follow

1. **One concept per note.** Each line (Life, Head, Heart, Fate, Sun, Mercury) gets its own note with the full traditional meaning, what to observe, and where traditions disagree. Lessons link to these rather than restating them — so there's one authoritative place per idea.
2. **Your words are untouched.** Notes under `04 - Reflections/` are never restructured or rewritten — only filed, dated, and linked.
3. **Practicing > reading.** Once we have a few hands, each reading gets its own note in `05 - Practice/` and an entry in the Reading Log. Honest "what I got wrong" matters more than a confident read.
4. **Traditions get attributed, not flattened.** Where Western classical and Indian practice disagree, I record both and flag it, rather than picking one silently.
5. **Claims stay labelled as claims.** Palmistry is a traditional interpretive practice, not validated science. Health/lifespan readings especially are recorded as *what the tradition says*, never as diagnosis. See [[00 - Course Overview]].
6. **Everything is linkable and tagged.** Wikilinks everywhere, plus `#palmistry/lesson`, `#palmistry/line`, `#palmistry/reference`, `#palmistry/journal`, `#palmistry/questions`, `#palmistry/practice`. Frontmatter (`title`, `tags`, `type`) on every note so Properties and Bases work.
7. **Your course wins.** If your course teaches something differently than I recorded, tell me and I'll match it. It's your notes.

## What I need from you to log a lesson
- The lesson name or number
- The topic
- Anything you want written down (notes, terms, questions, your reactions)

I'll create the note from the template, link it into the Home table, tag it, and update the Course Overview if the facts changed.
