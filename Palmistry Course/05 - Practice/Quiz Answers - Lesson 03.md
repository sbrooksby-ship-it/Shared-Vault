## Part A — Structure (5 points)

**1.** Depth, length, ending. *(1)*
**2.** **The ending** — where the line stops. *(1)*
**3.** Life, Head, Heart. *(1.5)*
**4.** Fate, Sun, Mercury. *(1.5)*
**5.** It prevents you from **convincing yourself of anything** — merging observation with interpretation means a vague impression feels like a hit. *(1)*

---

## Part B — Identification (6 points)

**6.** **Head line**, ending under the little finger → **logical, analytical, methodical; processes step by step.** *(2)*
**7.** **Heart line**, curving down → **reserved, controlled, holds feelings close.** *(2)*
   ⚠️ *Contested — Western classical adds "tendency toward melancholy." Indian Hasta Samudrika reads it as emotional depth/sensitivity. Either is defensible; say which school you're using.*
**8.** **Life line**, beaded → **vitality in bursts; highs and lows rather than steady.** *(2)*

---

## Part C — Application (6 points)

**9.** **Head line ending under the little finger.** *(1.5)*
**10.** **Heart line ending under the little finger.** *(1.5)*
**11.** **A short Life line in a FIRE hand.** *(1.5)*
**12.** **A short Life line in an EARTH hand.** *(1.5)*
   ⚠️ *Q11 and Q12 are the same line with opposite meanings. If you got one right and not the other, you know the rule; if you got both the same way, revisit [[Why Shape Comes First]].*

---

## Part D — Misconceptions (4 points)

**13.** **False.** The Life line does not predict lifespan. *(1)*
**14.** **Vitality** and **how you live the years you have.** *(1)*
**15.** **The Heart line curving downward.** *(2)*
- Western classical: tendency toward **melancholy**
- Indian (Hasta Samudrika): **emotional depth and sensitivity** — a strength

---

## Part E — The two-hands rule (2 points)

**16.** **Inherited material you haven't fully engaged yet.** *(1)*
**17.** **Do:** don't average them, don't pick one — **name the difference.** *(0.5)*
**It's called:** **tension** between what you came from and what you're doing about it. *(0.5)*

---

## Part F — Synthesis (2 points)

**18.** The reading order: *(1)*
1. **Shape & skin** — the frame
2. **Thumb** — drive and decision style
3. **Life line** — depth, length, ending
4. **Head line** — depth, slope, ending, start
5. **Heart line** — ending and curve
6. **Compare both hands** — where they agree and differ

**19.** The three causes: *(1)*
1. The line means something else than you thought
2. **You misread the placement** — go back to Lesson 2
3. The tradition is genuinely flexible / overstated
**You:** **write it down.** The misses are the most useful part — they show you where the tradition is weak, not where you failed.

---

## Part G — Applied case (2 points)

**20.** Model answer — *yours will differ in wording, which is fine:*

> The **sloping-down Head line ending under the index** describes an **imaginative, intuitive, big-picture mind** — it reaches for meaning and patterns rather than detail, and starting slightly below but joined to the Life line means **that imagination is balanced with bodily instinct rather than overriding it**. The **Heart line ending under the little finger and curving up** points to someone **emotionally deeply invested and openly warm** — relationships matter enormously and the feeling shows on the surface rather than being held back.

**Look for these three things in your own answer:**

| Element | Worth |
|---|---|
| Head line ending + slope → intuitive/big-picture mind | 0.75 |
| Head line *starting below and joined* → balanced with instinct | 0.5 |
| Heart line ending + curve → invested and openly warm | 0.75 |

*Also credit: naming that the **Earth shape** means the short Life line reads as "built for the long haul" rather than "runs hot." That's the premise from [[Why Shape Comes First]] showing up in practice.*
