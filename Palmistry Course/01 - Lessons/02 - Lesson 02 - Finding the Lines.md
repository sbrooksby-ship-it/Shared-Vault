# Lesson 02 — Finding the Lines

> **This lesson replaces the homework in Lesson 1.** That homework asked you to describe lines before I'd shown you where they are. Bad sequencing on my part. This is the missing lesson.

> **Goal:** by the end, you can put a finger on the Life, Head, and Heart lines of any hand without guessing. **No interpretation in this lesson at all.** Just finding them. Meaning comes later.

---

## The one landmark that solves everything

Before any line, find this spot. It is the starting point of two of the three major lines, and once you can find it you can find the rest.

**The landmark:** the fleshy valley **where the base of your thumb meets the side of your index finger, low down near the wrist.**

```
              index finger
                 │
    ╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎│
    ╎   the valley │  ← ← ← THIS SPOT. The base of the thumb where
    ╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎│        it meets the index finger.
     ╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎╎        Low down, near the wrist crease.
    ═══════════════   ← wrist crease
```

Put your own index fingertip there. That's the origin point. **The Life line and the Head line both begin at or very near this spot.**

## Why this is the trick
Palm lines are folds in the skin. They aren't random — they all anchor to this one region because that's where the skin has to flex most when you close your hand. Once you're anchored here, each line has a **specific direction** it runs, and you can only go one way.

## The three lines that always exist

### 1. The Life line — *the long curve around the thumb*

**How to find it:**
1. Start at the valley spot (above)
2. Run your finger **up and toward the thumb**
3. Keep going — it **curves around the base of your thumb**, hugging the fleshy pad
4. It usually ends near the thumb's base, or where the thumb joins the palm

```
                    valley
                      ●
                     ╱
    ┌───────────────╱
    │  the curve  ╱      ← this arc, wrapping around the
    │           ╱           fleshy base of the thumb
    │        ╱
    │     ╱
    │  ╱
    ╱
   thumb
```

**Tells:**
- It's the **longest** line
- It **curves** — it wraps the thumb pad
- It runs roughly **parallel to the outside edge of your thumb**

**Common mistakes:** people often look for the Life line in the *middle* of the palm. It isn't there. It's out at the **thumb side**, hugging the thumb.

---

### 2. The Head line — *the middle horizontal*

**How to find it:**
1. Go back to the valley spot
2. Your finger should now travel **sideways across the palm**, away from the thumb
3. It runs roughly **left to right across the middle** of the palm
4. It usually **meets or nearly touches** the Life line at the start, then runs out toward the little-finger edge
5. It **ends wherever it ends** — under the index, middle, ring, or little finger. That's its most-read feature, but don't worry about that yet

```
              valley
                ●──────────────────────────
                ╲                            ← HEAD line
                 ╲                            runs across the middle
    ┌─────────────╲
    │              ╲   ← it starts touching/near the Life line
    │   LIFE  ╱     ╲
    │   ╱           ╲
```

**Tells:**
- It **starts at the Life line** and immediately goes *sideways*
- It's the **middle** of the three horizontals
- It runs toward the **little-finger edge** (never toward the thumb)

**Common mistakes:** if a line starts somewhere in the middle of the palm with nothing at the valley, it is **not** the Head line. It's probably the [[Line - Fate|Fate line]] or a minor line.

---

### 3. The Heart line — *the topmost horizontal*

**How to find it:**
1. Look **under the index finger**, at the base of it
2. From there, run your finger **sideways across the palm**
3. It's the **first horizontal line** you meet going down from the fingers
4. It ends somewhere toward the little-finger edge

```
   ───────────────────────────────────────   ← HEART line
                     ↑
              starts under the index
    ──────────────────────────────────────    ← HEAD line
                ╲
                 ╲  LIFE line
```

**Tells:**
- It's the **highest** of the three horizontals
- It starts under the **index finger**, at the fleshy base of it
- It runs in the **same sideways direction** as the Head line

**The trap:** Heart and Head lines can **run very close together**, almost touching, in some hands. If you see two horizontals bunched up, the **upper one is Heart**, the lower is Head. If they're merged into one thick band, that's a notable feature — we'll cover it later.

## The three that are optional

These you look for, but often won't find. **Not finding one is normal, not a failure.**

### 4. The Fate line — *the vertical*

```
                │  ← toward the MIDDLE finger
                │
    ────────────┼──────────
                │  ← toward the RING finger = that's the SUN line
    ────────────┼──────────
                │
```

**How to find it:** start at the **wrist crease** (where the arm meets the palm) and look for a line going **straight up the middle** of the palm. It heads for the **middle finger**. It may start at the wrist, or begin halfway up the palm — both happen.

### 5. The Sun line — *another vertical, next door*

Same idea, but it runs up under the **ring finger** (the "Sun" finger). Often sits beside the Fate line rather than alone. Frequently absent.

### 6. The Mercury line — *short, at the edge*

Look on the **outer edge, under the little finger**. A short horizontal-ish line. Rare, and easy to miss entirely.

---

## The wrist creases — *your orientation reference*

At the very base of the palm, above the heel, there are usually **two or three horizontal creases** running across the wrist. These are the **bracelets / rascette**.

```
    ═══════════════════   ← crease 1
    ═══════════════════   ← crease 2
    ═══════════════════   ← crease 3
         ↑ the heel of the hand
```

**Why you need them:** they're your **bottom edge**. Every line you just learned either starts at or above these creases. If a line appears to start *below* them, it's in the heel of the hand and it's not one of the majors.

## Making it findable — the technique, again

You've got the anatomy. Now the technique, which is what actually makes lines *visible*:

| Step | Why |
|---|---|
| **Warm the hand** | Cold hands genuinely show fainter lines. Warm water or cupped hands |
| **Dry it well** | Dirt and moisture hide fine lines |
| **Let it go completely limp** | Cupping the palm fakes depth. Ask: *"Let your hand go floppy and lay it flat"* |
| **Raking side light** | A lamp **beside** and slightly above the hand. Shadows are what make creases visible — overhead flash flattens them |
| **Trace, don't just stare** | Run your fingertip along where you expect a line. Your finger finds ridges your eye misses |
| **Check from two angles** | Tilt the hand. A real crease stays put |

## 📝 Homework — achievable this time

**Nothing to interpret. Nothing to write about meanings. Just find things.**

Do this on **your own left hand** (or your dominant hand), in this order:

- [ ] **1.** Find the valley — thumb base where it meets the index. *Got it? Stop and check it feels right.*
- [ ] **2.** From the valley, trace **outward and up around the thumb.** Found the Life line. **Trace its whole path to where it ends.** Notice: does it end at the thumb base? Partway up? Under the index?
- [ ] **3.** Back to the valley, trace **sideways across the middle.** That's the Head line. Trace it to its end. *Where does it stop — under the index, middle, ring, or little finger?* Just note it.
- [ ] **4.** Look **under the index finger** and find the **topmost** horizontal. That's the Heart line. Trace it to its end.
- [ ] **5.** Look at the **wrist creases.** How many?
- [ ] **6.** Look for a **vertical line up the middle** toward the middle finger. *(Fate — may not be there. That's fine.)*
- [ ] **7.** Look for a vertical under the **ring finger**. *(Sun — may not be there.)*
- [ ] **8.** Look under the **little finger** for a short line. *(Mercury — rare, don't worry.)*

**That's the whole assignment.** Eight things, none of which require you to understand any meaning.

**Then tell me just this:** which of steps 2–4 did you *not* find? That's the one I'll teach next.
